# Other papers and references

## The Data and Digital Object Management Plans (D(DO)MPs)

This paper explores the role of Data and Digital Object Management Plans (D(DO)MPs) in promoting data sharing in multidisciplinary and multinational research projects. It discusses how a structured approach to managing research data and related digital objects can support collaboration, improve data management practices and facilitate data sharing across different research contexts.

[Read the paper](https://datascience.codata.org/articles/10.5334/dsj-2023-038)

## Ten Simple Rules for Creating a Good Data Management Plan

This paper presents ten practical recommendations for developing an effective data management plan (DMP). It provides advice on how to structure a plan, address key data management considerations and make the plan useful throughout the research lifecycle. It is a useful introduction for researchers who are new to DMP writing, as well as a reference for those looking to review and improve their existing plans.

[Read the paper](https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/article?id=10.1371/journal.pcbi.1004525)

## Towards a Toolbox for Automated Assessment of Machine-Actionable Data Management Plans

This research paper explores how the assessment of DMPs could be supported by automated approaches.

The authors discuss how machine-actionable DMPs (maDMPs) and semantic technologies can be used to analyse plan content, provide preliminary feedback and retrieve additional information to help reviewers assess proposed data management practices. The paper is a useful resource for understanding the potential benefits and challenges of automating DMP assessment.

[Read the paper](https://datascience.codata.org/articles/10.5334/dsj-2023-028)
