Examples of published DMPs can help researchers as a source of inspiration for their own DMPs. This section is particularly useful for researchers looking for discipline-specific examples rather than generic templates. The catalogues and repositories below provide access to plans and examples from different research fields and countries. Some offer curated collections of DMPs, while others are general-purpose repositories in which plans can be discovered through searches.
When using these examples, it is important to remember that DMPs are context-dependent. Content always needs to be adapted to the project's research objectives, data types, ethical and legal requirements, institutional policies and funder expectations. Note also that the listed DMPs have not necessarily been assessed for quality or completeness, so their relevance and suitability should be evaluated individually. 

## ARGOS
[ARGOS](https://argos.openaire.eu/home) is an OpenAIRE platform for creating, managing and publishing DMPs (add internal links). In addition to supporting the development of new plans, it allows users to browse publicly available DMPs without signing in. 

## Zenodo
Zenodo is a general-purpose repository for research outputs rather than a dedicated DMP catalogue. Nevertheless, it [contains many published DMPs](https://zenodo.org/search?q=Data%20Management%20Plan&l=list&p=1&s=10&sort=bestmatch), including project deliverables and the DMPs deposited through ARGOS. Search results can be filtered and explored to find examples relevant to a particular discipline, funder or research project.

## LIBER Data Management Plan Catalogue
The [LIBER Research Data Management Working Group's DMP Catalogue](https://libereurope.eu/working-group/research-data-management/plans/) brings together published DMPs from different disciplines and research contexts. It provides access to examples of plans alongside quality reviews intended to help researchers and research support professionals identify useful practices and approaches.

The catalogue includes a review matrix based on elements of the Digital Curation Centre's DMP assessment and guidance resources. It covers key aspects of data management planning, including data collection, documentation and metadata, ethical and legal considerations, storage and backup, preservation and data sharing.

The catalogue can therefore be used both to explore examples of published DMPs and to understand how plans can be reviewed systematically. Its assessments are intended to support learning and provide inspiration, rather than to serve as formal or authoritative judgements of the plans' quality.

## Digital Curation Centre (DCC): Example DMPs and Guidance
The [Digital Curation Centre's collection of example DMPs and guidance brings together DMPs and supporting resources](https://www.dcc.ac.uk/resources/data-management-plans/guidance-examples) from different funders, institutions and research disciplines. Examples are organised by funding body and include plans from a range of research contexts, alongside selected assessment rubrics and guidance for reviewers.
This collection is particularly useful when preparing a DMP for a specific funding scheme, as it can help researchers understand the level of detail and types of information expected. The DCC also provides practical guidance on developing DMPs. 

## Disciplinary Data Management Plans (RDA France)
The [Corpus of Disciplinary Data Management Plans](https://entrepot.recherche.data.gouv.fr/dataset.xhtml?persistentId=doi:10.57745/ZSWLYJ) was compiled by RDA France, the French chapter of the Research Data Alliance. It is a spreadsheet that brings together publicly available DMPs. The collection includes basic metadata and direct link to the DMPs. The resource is primarily in French, although some English-DMP are also available.
