# Other guides to write DMPs

This guide is not the only resource available to help you get started with writing a DMP or support you throughout the process.

Over the years, several initiatives have developed their own guidance, tailored to specific needs, contexts or approaches to research data management.

We have collected a selection of these resources to help you find the approach that best suits your needs and explore different perspectives on DMP writing. They include practical guides, checklists and recommendations to help you draft, review and improve your DMP in line with good RDM practices.

The resources listed here are provided for information purposes only. Their inclusion does not imply endorsement or recommendation of any particular resource. This is a non-exhaustive list that may be expanded and updated over time.

## Leiden checklist and DMP template
Leiden University is among the first and most authoritative institutions that take Data Stewardship and RDM seriously. They developed several useful resources. Among all, you can find useful their [Tips and tricks for writing a Data Management Plan](https://zenodo.org/records/4312894), which provides practical advice on developing a DMP. 

Building on this guidance, the university has also developed [its own DMP template, available in Word (.docx) format](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.3903267) and freely accessible for anyone to use.

## Practical Guide to the International Alignment of Research Data Management

The [Practical Guide to the International Alignment of Research Data Management](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.4915861) is developed by Science Europe.

The extended edition includes a rubric to support the assessment of DMPs, outlines core requirements, and provides recommendations for selecting trustworthy repositories and meeting organisational requirements.

## UCL Research Data Management: Best Practices and How-to Guides

University College London (UCL) provides a [collection of practical guides and recommendations on RDM](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/library/research-support/research-data-management/best-practices/how-guides). The resources cover different aspects of managing research data and offer actionable advice to help researchers address data management challenges throughout the research lifecycle.

## Digital Curation Centre (DCC): How to Develop a Data Management Plan

The Digital Curation Centre (DCC) provides [practical guidance on developing a DMP](https://www.dcc.ac.uk/guidance/how-guides/develop-data-plan). The guide helps researchers understand the main elements of a DMP and identify the issues to consider when planning how research data will be managed, documented, shared and preserved.
