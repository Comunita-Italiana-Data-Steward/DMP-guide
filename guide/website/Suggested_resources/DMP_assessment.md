# DMP assessment

Assessing a DMP can help researchers, data stewards and funders identify strengths, gaps and areas for improvement. The resources below include assessment rubrics, review frameworks, practical guidance on the assessment of machine-actionable DMPs (maDMPs).

They support different purposes, from reviewing individual plans against established criteria to exploring how assessment can be made more consistent, transparent and scalable.

## Science Europe: Research Data Management

The [Practical Guide to the International Alignment of Research Data Management](https://scienceeurope.org/our-resources/practical-guide-to-the-international-alignment-of-research-data-management/) is an extended edition of the Science Europe guidance. It provides guidance and templates to support RDM, including resources for researchers, research organisations and DMP reviewers.

The resources include a section on core requirements for DMPs, a series of criteria to select a trustworthy repository for the data, a template derived from such requirements, and an evaluation grid which can help both reviewers evaluate the completeness and quality of a plan, while providing researchers with a clearer understanding of what is expected.

## FAIR-Aware: Additional Guidance to the Science Europe DMP Assessment Rubric

The [FAIR-Aware additional guidance](https://zenodo.org/records/6088215), developed through the FAIRsFAIR project, supplements the Science Europe DMP assessment rubric with a more explicit focus on the FAIR principles: Findable, Accessible, Interoperable and Reusable.

It provides additional guidance to help researchers and data stewards consider FAIR-related aspects when preparing or reviewing a DMP. It is intended to complement, rather than replace, the original Science Europe guidance.


## Belmont Forum Data Management Plan Scorecard

In the words of the authors *This document is a resource developed for the Belmont Forum for evaluating formal Data Management Plans submitted by research teams who've been awarded Collaborative Research Action funding.*

*The purpose and use of this Scorecard is three-fold:*

*1.	as an evaluation tool to quantitatively assess full proposal DMP responses to questions drawn from the Data and Digital Outputs Management Plan (DDOMP) template that are included in the Belmont Forum grant application process on BFGO.org;*

*2.	as a training resource to be shared with potential proposers to help define expectations for data management and/or with awardees (i.e., post-award) to identify specific elements of their data management planning efforts that may be unclear or lacking; and/or*
   
*3.	as an evaluation tool employed by the Secretariat or TPOs to access milestones and progress during mid- or end-term project review/valorization.*

The [Belmont Forum DMP scorecard](https://zenodo.org/records/3530933) provides a structured approach to assessing DMP responses against defined criteria. It can be used to evaluate DMPs but also as a checklist to resarchers that are writing a DMP.

## DCC Horizon 2020 DMP Compliance rubric
[This framework](http://www.dcc.ac.uk/sites/default/files/documents/resource/DMP/H2020%20DMP%20compliance%20rubric.pdf) is based on the initial template for a DMP listed in Annex 1 of the European Commission Guidelines on Data Management in Horizon 2020. The method adopted is based on the evaluation rubric approach developed as part of the DART project. It was developed by the UK Digital Curation Centre. See also the “[DCC Checklist for a Data Management Plan v4](http://www.dcc.ac.uk/sites/default/files/documents/resource/DMP/DMP-checklist-flyer.pdf)”
